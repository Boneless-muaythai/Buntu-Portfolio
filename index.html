Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take to build?

Most projects launch in 2–4 weeks depending on scope and how quickly content and feedback come through. You'll get a clear timeline before we begin, so there are no surprises.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your logo and brand assets (if you have them), any copy or images you'd like included, and a few examples of sites you like. No content ready yet? No problem — I'll guide you through it step by step.

Do I own the website once it's finished?

Yes — 100%. Once your project is paid in full, you own the design, the code, and every file. There's no lock-in and no hidden licensing.

Can you help with hosting and domains?

Absolutely. I'll help you choose and set up hosting, connect your domain, and get everything live. I can also manage it for you or hand over full control — whichever you prefer.

What happens if I need changes after launch?

Each package includes revisions during the build. After launch, I offer ongoing support and maintenance plans if you'd like a hand keeping your site fresh, secure, and up to date.
